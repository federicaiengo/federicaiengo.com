# Notion public-site baseline — v1

Date: 2026-10-07

## Scope
Independent portfolio research using public pages. This is not commissioned by or affiliated with Notion.

## Observable positioning
- Primary product positioning: an AI/connected workspace combining docs, wikis, projects and databases.
- AI positioning includes agents, enterprise search and meeting notes.
- Project-management pages expose concrete task/subtask, status, assignee, due date, views, dependencies and progress concepts.
- Enterprise Search describes permission-aware indexing/search across connected tools including Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Jira, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive.
- Public pricing exposes Free, Plus, Business and Enterprise tiers; the observed localized page lists Plus at USD 10/member/month and Business at USD 20/member/month, with Enterprise custom pricing.

## Initial AEO/GEO observations
1. Strong answerability: product pages contain explicit question-and-answer sections that resolve high-intent questions in concise language.
2. Strong entity/capability language: product, feature and connector names are explicit rather than implied.
3. Strong comparison-ready evidence: pricing and feature packaging are exposed in structured plan sections.
4. Potential measurement challenge: broad positioning spans workspace, project management, enterprise search and agents. Visibility should therefore be measured by intent family, not as one generic 'Notion visibility' score.

## Evidence discipline
These are observations about public content structure. They are not claims about Notion's actual AI-search ranking, traffic or conversion performance. Those require separate measurement.
