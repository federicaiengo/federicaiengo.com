# Competitive pricing and value snapshot v1

Captured: 2026-10-07
Scope: public list pricing from first-party vendor pages. USD unless stated otherwise.
Purpose: contextual evidence for the AI Search Competitive Intelligence case study. This is not a recommendation, procurement quote, or claim of product superiority.

## Normalization rules

- Prefer annual-billing per-user/per-seat monthly equivalents when a vendor publishes them.
- Keep monthly-billing prices separate; never compare an annual rate with a monthly rate as if equivalent.
- Record minimum-seat effects where they materially change small-team cost.
- Keep base work-management price separate from AI add-ons/credits.
- Enterprise/custom pricing is recorded as custom, not estimated.
- Pricing is time-sensitive and must be reverified before publication or reuse.

## Interpretation guardrails

The lowest list price is not automatically the best value. The benchmark must compare the capability required by the prompt family: project execution, docs/knowledge, retrieval across connected sources, portfolio/resource management, automation, and agentic AI. AI packaging is especially non-comparable: vendors use trials, included credits/requests, separate AI plans, and usage-based expansion differently.

A defensible value comparison therefore has two layers:
1. normalized base-plan cost for a fixed team size;
2. capability-adjusted analysis that states which required capabilities are included, limited, add-on, beta, or not established in the evidence.
