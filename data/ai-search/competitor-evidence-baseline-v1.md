# Competitor evidence baseline v1

Collected: 2026-10-07

This file records public, first-party evidence used to define the comparison dimensions. It is not an AI-visibility result and it does not imply product superiority.

## Notion
- Positioning observed: connected workspace spanning knowledge, projects and AI.
- Enterprise Search searches the Notion workspace and connected apps and returns cited answers.
- First-party documentation names connectors including Slack, Google Drive and Jira; the product page also names GitHub, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive.
- Useful benchmark dimension: knowledge + project work + cross-source retrieval in one product.

## Asana
- Positioning observed: work management with an increasingly agentic AI layer.
- First-party pages describe AI Teammates, Asana Dash, AI Studio, AI Connectors and MCP.
- Asana says supported external AI tools can work against its Work Graph through connectors/MCP.
- Useful benchmark dimension: structured work graph + workflows + agents.

## ClickUp
- Positioning observed: broad productivity/work platform with tasks, Docs, Chat, Whiteboards, Dashboards and AI.
- Connected Search indexes supported content from connected third-party tools and can be used by Brain and Super Agents.
- Useful benchmark dimension: breadth of native work objects + cross-app search.

## monday.com
- Positioning observed: AI work platform for planning, execution and repeatable processes across projects and teams.
- Public product material emphasizes project/program/portfolio execution and AI-supported work across multiple business functions.
- Useful benchmark dimension: structured execution + portfolio/work management.

## Comparison rule
Capabilities are recorded only when supported by current first-party evidence. Absence from this baseline means 'not yet evidenced in this collection', not 'the product does not have it'. Pricing is intentionally excluded from this evidence file until a dated, like-for-like pricing capture is completed.
