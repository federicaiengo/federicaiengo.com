# AI Search Measurement Principles

## Do not confuse visibility with ranking
An AI response is synthesized from a changing retrieval and generation process. A brand mention in one answer is an observation, not a stable rank.

## Separate the layers
Measure mention coverage, citation coverage, recommendation inclusion, source patterns and consistency independently before producing any composite score.

## Preserve prompt context
Prompts are grouped by buyer intent: discovery, alternatives, comparison, capabilities, pricing/value and decision-stage recommendation.

## Separate evidence from interpretation
Every case study distinguishes observed public evidence, analysis/inference, proposed intervention and post-intervention measurement.

## Correlation is not causation
A relationship between mentions, citations, content structure or third-party presence and AI visibility is treated as a signal to test, not an automatic ranking factor.
